What’s inside

Everything you need to calculate

From basic arithmetic to scientific functions and selected engineering tools — in one clean, focused app.

Scientific Calculator
sin, cos, tan, log, ln, powers, roots, factorial — with a structured visual math editor that shows expressions clearly.
Calculation History
Every calculation is saved locally. Browse, recall, and reuse past expressions — all stored privately on your device.
5 Customizable Themes
Choose from Domino, Graphite, Midnight, Porcelain, and Sandstone. Every surface adapts cleanly across all themes.
Health & Science Tools
BMI and BMR calculators, unit conversion, area calculations, and selected statistics tools — for study and everyday use.
Local Text Recognition
Point your camera at a handwritten or printed expression and import it directly into the editor. Processed entirely on-device — nothing is uploaded.
Statistics & Probability
Descriptive statistics, one-sample t-test, sample size estimation, and more — for coursework and data review.

Private by design

CalcEditor stores everything locally on your device. No accounts. No cloud sync. No analytics. No ads. Your calculations, history, and health data stay with you.
